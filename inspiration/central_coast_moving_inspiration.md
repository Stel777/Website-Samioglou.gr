## Useful Patterns

- Practical quote form in or near the hero.
- Local-service feel rather than flashy marketing.
- Trustworthy blue accent works well for moving-company credibility.
- Clear utility sections.
- Proof strips and operational detail make the company feel grounded.

## Layout Signals

- No horizontal overflow observed on desktop or mobile.
- Large hero area with form.
- Multiple practical sections, likely from a page-builder structure.
- Calm, work-focused visual hierarchy.
- Less decorative than the other references.

## What To Borrow Conceptually

- Put the quote form near the top.
- Use simple service explanations and direct CTAs.
- Keep layout practical and readable.
- Make the business feel local and reachable.

## What To Avoid

- Avoid generic page-builder blandness.
- Do not let utility become visually flat.
- Do not copy exact layout blocks or wording.
- Avoid underusing imagery.
