# SOS Moving LA Inspiration Notes

Source: https://www.sosmovingla.net/

Use this file for structural inspiration only. Do not copy competitor text, reviews, claims, prices, phone numbers, or service wording.

## Useful Patterns

- Clear long-form homepage flow.
- Repeated CTA opportunities throughout the page.
- Service areas and locations are treated as important content, not an afterthought.
- Reviews/social proof sections make the page feel active.
- FAQ appears late in the page to answer doubts before conversion.
- Bottom CTA creates a strong final action point.

## Layout Signals

- No horizontal overflow observed on desktop or mobile.
- Many full-width sections.
- Strong use of service-area and services sections.
- FAQ and bottom CTA are major conversion elements.
- Heavy image/review/service-card density.

## What To Borrow Conceptually

- Use a clear section order:
  hero, intro, services, service areas, why choose us, FAQ, final CTA.
- Repeat quote/contact CTAs after important sections.
- Give service areas a dedicated, usable section.
- Use FAQ as a conversion support tool.

## What To Avoid

- Do not copy the review content or testimonial structure literally.
- Avoid an overly long mobile page with too many repeated sliders.
- Avoid heavy image galleries unless Samioglou has strong real photos.
- Do not overload the prototype with every possible section.

## Samioglou Translation

Use SOS as the model for content rhythm. Samioglou should have enough sections to feel complete, but each section should be tighter and easier to scan.
