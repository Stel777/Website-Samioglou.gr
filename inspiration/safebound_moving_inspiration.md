# Safebound Moving Inspiration Notes

Source: https://www.safeboundmoving.com/

Use this file for structural inspiration only. Do not copy competitor text, reviews, claims, prices, phone numbers, or service wording.

## Useful Patterns

- Best reference for the service-type image mosaic.
- Varied card sizes create visual interest.
- Image-first service cards communicate quickly.
- Header contact CTA feels immediate and human.
- Overall polish is high without relying on complex decoration.
- Large visual sections make the brand feel established.

## Layout Signals

- No horizontal overflow observed on desktop or mobile.
- Strong image/card density.
- Service cards and visual sections dominate the experience.
- Header is compact.
- Quote/contact action remains important.

## What To Borrow Conceptually

- Build a service mosaic for Samioglou moving types.
- Use real moving images as card backgrounds.
- Vary card sizes on desktop.
- Stack cards cleanly on mobile.
- Use concise labels on top of imagery.

## What To Avoid

- Do not copy exact service card composition.
- Do not copy their labels, brand tone, or visual identity.
- Avoid over-rounding cards beyond the local design system.
- Avoid making the mosaic too tall on mobile.
